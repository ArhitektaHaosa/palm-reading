# How to photograph a palm

The report is written from what is visible in the picture. A clear phone photo in daylight is enough.

One hand is $10 and needs one photograph. Two hands are $20 and need two photographs. Pay only at https://arhitektahaosa.gumroad.com/l/palmreading.

## Do

- Stand by a window. Even daylight. Creases should stay dark against the skin.
- Open the palm. Fingers slightly apart, not clenched, not stretched until the skin distorts.
- Shoot from above, as if cataloguing an object. Include the wrist.
- One file per hand. JPEG, PNG, or WebP. Under 10 MB.
- If you send two hands, say which is left, which is right, and which is dominant.

## Do not

- Beauty mode, portrait smoothing, or filters
- Hard flash on the skin (it often wipes the lines out)
- Crop at the base of the thumb
- Jewelry covering the palm
- A screenshot of someone else’s hand

If the first picture is unreadable, the desk asks for another before the two working days start.
