## This is not a science

This is an entertainment reading of palm lines from a photograph. Palmistry is folklore. It does not predict the future. It is not medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice. The length of a life line is not a lifespan. Do not make decisions about health, money, law, or safety from this text.
