# Fulfillment desk

Operator notes. Not customer-facing.

## Clock

Two working days = Monday–Friday after **both** of these have arrived:

1. A successful Gumroad payment at https://arhitektahaosa.gumroad.com/l/palmreading. $10 for one hand. $20 for two hands. No PayPal.
2. A usable palm photograph emailed to `mapkomah@gmail.com`, with the Gumroad sale ID in the subject. One photograph for $10. Two photographs for $20.

Weekends and public holidays do not count. If the photo is unreadable, the clock starts when a readable replacement arrives.

## Match

1. Gumroad: paid sale, sale ID, buyer email, amount. $10 is one hand. $20 is two hands.
2. Email: photograph count must match the amount. Sale ID in the subject.
3. If there is payment and no photo, wait. Do not invent a reading. One reminder email is enough.
4. If there is a photo and no payment, do not write the report.
5. If $10 arrived and two hands were sent, read one hand and say so. If $20 arrived and one hand was sent, wait for the second photograph.

## Write

Use [REPORT-TEMPLATE.md](REPORT-TEMPLATE.md). English only. Disclaimer first. No lifespan from the life line. No medical, legal, or money claims.

## Send

Email the report to the address on the Gumroad receipt, unless the customer named a different address. Subject: `Palm Desk reading — {SALE_ID}`.

## After

Discard the photographs when the report has been sent. Do not publish them. Do not feed them to a model.

## If they ask “is this real?”

Answer with the disclaimer. It is entertainment. It is not a science.
